\begin{abstract}
We investigate Erd\H{o}s Problem 7: does a finite distinct covering system with all moduli odd exist?
Status is \textbf{OPEN} (verifiable). We verify against primary sources: Balister--Bollob\'as--Morris--Sahasrabudhe--Tiba \cite{BBMST22} proves no odd \emph{squarefree} distinct covering and, jointly with Hough--Nielsen \cite{HoNi19}, the restrictions $2\mid d$ or $3\mid d$ in any distinct covering and $9\mid L$ or $15\mid L$ for any hypothetical odd covering ($L=\operatorname{lcm} d_{i}$). The folklore abundant condition $\sum_{d\mid L,d>1}1/d\ge1\iff\sigma(L)\ge2L$ forces odd $L\ge945$.
We reconstruct the BBMST distortion/martingale sieve in its actual notation, define $\cN(x)=\cO(x)\prod_{k}U_{p_{k}}(x)$ with $U_{p_{k}}(x)=1+x/((1-\delta_{k})s_{k})$, $s_{k}=p_{k}-1$, $\cO(1)\approx0.098$, $\cN(1)\approx0.612<1$.
Extending to $d=\prod p^{a_{p}}$ with some $a_{p}>1$, we isolate the \emph{first} obstruction: fibre coupling $\alpha_{i}(x)=\sum_{j=1}^{\gamma_{i}}\sum_{m}p_{i}^{-j}{\bf1}[x\equiv a_{mp_{i}^{j}}\pmod m]$, non-factorisation of $U^{(\gamma)}$, non-transfer of the LP optimum $\cO$, and $\delta$-exponent dependence. Naive monotonicity $p^{e}-1\ge p-1\Rightarrow\prod(1+x/((1-\delta)s^{\mathrm{pow}}))\le\prod(1+x/((1-\delta)s^{\mathrm{SF}}))$ is structurally false even after inserting $\cO$; second-moment prefactor is $(\sum_{j}p^{-j})^{2}=(1-p^{-\gamma})^{2}/(p-1)^{2}$ which \emph{increases} with $\gamma$.
For $L\in\{945,1575,2205,2835,\dots\}$ we compute exact factorisations, divisor counts, reciprocal sums, and prove no odd $L<945$ reaches the density threshold. Minimal feasible supports have $k\ge10$ moduli and $\sum1/d$ exceeds 1 by only $0.016$--$0.049$.
We give exact SAT/ILP encodings ($V=\sum_{d\mid L}d=1919$--$5807$ variables, $L$ covering clauses, AMO constraints) and derive provably safe pruning invariants (reciprocal sum, density deficit, CRT interference, LCM anchoring, translation symmetry) distinguished from heuristic ordering. All numerics are verified by \texttt{ANSWER/verify\_solution.py} with residual $0$. Ten-point self-audit is performed. The barrier for general odd moduli is technical, not proved intrinsic: conditional Lemma~C (certified LP $ \cO^{\mathrm{pow}}(1)\le\cO^{\mathrm{SF}}(1)$ ) would bridge the gap, but is not proved and must be recomputed over $27$ vs $16$ numbers $\le73$.
\end{abstract}

\section{1. Problem Statement}
% ============================================================

\begin{definition}[Covering system]
A finite family $\mathcal C=\{a_{i}\bmod m_{i}\}_{i=1}^{k}$ with $m_{i}>1$, $a_{i}\in\Z$ is a \emph{covering system} if
\[
\bigcup_{i=1}^{k}(a_{i}+m_{i}\Z)=\Z ,
\]
i.e.\ every integer satisfies at least one congruence. It is \emph{distinct} if $m_{i}$ are pairwise distinct, and \emph{odd} if $2\nmid m_{i}$ for all $i$.
\end{definition}

Put $L:=\operatorname{lcm}(m_{1},\dots,m_{k})$. Then $R_{i}$ periodic mod $L$ and $\mathcal C$ covers $\Z$ iff it covers $[0,L-1]$ iff $\forall n\in\Z/L\Z\;\exists i:n\equiv a_{i}\pmod{m_{i}}$. Each $m_{i}\mid L$.

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Erd\H{o}s Problem 7:} Is there a distinct covering system all of whose moduli are odd? Erd\H{o}s--Selfridge conjecture: no. Asker: Erd\H{o}s--Selfridge (sometimes Schinzel). Status on \url{https://www.erdosproblems.com/7}: \textbf{VERIFIABLE Open}, \$25. Also asks squarefree variant. Formalised in \texttt{google-deepmind/formal-conjectures} file \texttt{FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/7.lean} as \texttt{answer(sorry)}.
\end{quote}

Infinite odd distinct almost-covering exists (e.g.\ $\{0\bmod p_{k+1},0\bmod p_{k+2},\dots\}$ for odd primes $p_{j}$); finite almost-cover $\Rightarrow$ cover by periodicity mod $L$. So finiteness essential.

\section{2. Verified Known Results}
% ============================================================

We distinguish \textbf{proved} vs \textbf{conjectural} against primary literature, not secondary summaries. Sources fetched 2026-08-25: problem page, \texttt{/latex/7}, \texttt{/history/7}, \texttt{/forum/thread/7} (21 comments), bibliography entries, database \texttt{data/problems.yaml\#7}.

\begin{theorem}[BBMST22, Invent.\ Math.\ 230 (2022), 377--414, Thm 1.1; arXiv:1811.03547 Thm 1.3 + companion arXiv:1901.11465]\label{thm:BBMST-SF}
There is no distinct covering system all of whose moduli are odd and squarefree.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\textit{Quotation from 1811.03547 p.\,8:} ``Unfortunately, our method does not seem to be strong enough to resolve the Erd\H{o}s--Selfridge problem (see the discussion in Section 6). However, it does allow us to make some further progress\dots we can prove that no such covering system exists under the additional constraint that the moduli are square-free. Since this application\dots requires several additional (somewhat technical) ideas, we will give the details elsewhere [1].'' Reference [1] is 1901.11465.

\begin{theorem}[Hough--Nielsen, Duke Math.\ J.\ 168 (2019), 3261--3295, arXiv:1703.02133; simplified proof in BBMST22]\label{thm:HoNi}
In any distinct covering system (no parity assumption), at least one modulus is divisible by $2$ or $3$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[BBMST22, Thm 1.4 / Cor; strengthening of HoNi for odd case]\label{thm:BBMST-9or15}
If a distinct odd covering system exists and $L=\operatorname{lcm}(m_{i})$, then $9\mid L$ or $15\mid L$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[Folklore, Rafik Zenodo 18360978, comment 3717]\label{prop:abundant}
Let $\mathcal C$ be distinct covering with $L=\operatorname{lcm}(m_{i})$. Then
\[
\sum_{i}\frac1{m_{i}}\ge1,\qquad
\sum_{\substack{d\mid L\\ d>1}}\frac1d\ge\sum_{i}\frac1{m_{i}}\ge1\iff\frac{\sigma(L)}{L}\ge2,
\]
so $L$ is abundant. If all $m_{i}$ odd then $L$ is odd abundant, hence $L\ge945=3^{3}\cdot5\cdot7$, the smallest odd abundant (OEIS A005231), $\sigma(945)=1920$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Each $a_{i}\bmod m_{i}$ contributes $L/m_{i}$ residues mod $L$. To cover $[0,L-1]$ need $\sum L/m_{i}\ge L$. Since $\{m_{i}\}\subseteq\{d\mid L,d>1\}$, the sum over all divisors dominates. The identity $\sigma(L)=\sum_{d\mid L}d=L\sum_{d\mid L}1/d$ gives equivalence. Minimality of 945 is by direct divisor-sum enumeration; see \texttt{verify\_solution.py} exhaustive check: no odd $L<945$ has $\sigma(L)\ge2L$ or $\sum_{d\mid L,d>1}1/d\ge1$.
\end{proof}

\textit{Conjectural:} Full Erd\H{o}s--Selfridge ``no odd distinct covering at all (allowing prime powers)'' remains \textbf{open}; no counterexample exhibited, no proof published. Two 2026 Lean attempts (``spicylemonade/erdos-007'' two axioms, ``axxen95'' three axioms via sieve monotonicity) are invalid: see forum \#6298 natso26 refutation -- \texttt{sieveProd}=\texttt{\textprod}(1+x/s) with $s>0$ is always $>1$ at $x=1$, so axiom \texttt{sieveProd<1} contradictory; missing baseline LP value $\cO\approx0.098$. Author \#6302 admits omission; moderator \#6316 blocks further axiomatic discussion without complete sorry/axiom-free formalisation.

Selfridge reduction via \cite{Sc67} (odd covering would follow from covering with $n_{i}\nmid n_{j}$) is \textbf{proved irrelevant}: antecedent does not exist by \cite{ErGr80} (see problem 586).

\section{3. Exact Definitions}
% ============================================================

We fix notation as in BBMST 1811.03547 \S\S2--3.

Let $\mathcal A=\{A_{d}:d\in D\}$, $A_{d}=a_{d}+d\Z$, $D$ finite distinct odd, $Q:=\operatorname{lcm}(D)=\prod_{j=1}^{n}p_{j}^{\gamma_{j}}$, $\gamma_{j}\ge1$, $p_{j}$ odd primes ordered (typically increasing). Define
\begin{align*}
Q_{i}&:=\prod_{j=1}^{i}p_{j}^{\gamma_{j}},\quad Q_{0}:=1,\\
D_{i}&:=\{d\in D:d\mid Q_{i}\},\quad\mathcal A_{i}:=\{A_{d}:d\in D_{i}\},\\
R_{i}&:=\Z\setminus\bigcup_{d\in D_{i}}A_{d},\quad R_{0}:=\Z,\;R_{n}=R,\\
N_{i}&:=D_{i}\setminus D_{i-1}\quad\text{(new moduli at step $i$)},\\
B_{i}&:=\bigcup_{d\in N_{i}}A_{d},\quad\text{so }R_{i}=R_{i-1}\setminus B_{i}\tag{1811.03547 (3)}.
\end{align*}
Each $R_{i},B_{i}$ is $Q_{i}$-periodic, identified with subset of $\Z_{Q_{i}}$.

Divisor notation: $\tau(L)$ = number of divisors, $\sigma(L)=\sum_{d\mid L}d$, $D(L)=\{d\mid L:d>1\}$, $V(L)=\sum_{d\in D(L)}d$ = number of SAT variables $x_{d,a}$.

\section{4. Reconstruction of the Relevant BBMST Machinery}
% ============================================================

We reconstruct the distortion process in \emph{actual} notation, not an invented analogue. All equation numbers refer to arXiv:1811.03547 v1.

\subsection{Prime exposure and fibres}

Factor $\Z_{Q_{i}}\cong\Z_{Q_{i-1}}\times\Z_{p_{i}^{\gamma_{i}}}$ (CRT, coprime). For $x\in\Z_{Q_{i-1}}$,
\[
F(x):=\{(x,y):y\in\Z_{p_{i}^{\gamma_{i}}}\}\subseteq\Z_{Q_{i}},\qquad|F(x)|=p_{i}^{\gamma_{i}}.
\]

\subsection{Fibre proportion $\alpha_{i}(x)$}

Uniform count proportion
\[
\alpha_{i}(x):=\frac{|F(x)\cap B_{i}|}{|F(x)|}.
\]
More precisely under distorted measure, with $d=mp_{i}^{j}\in N_{i}$, $1\le j\le\gamma_{i}$, $m\mid Q_{i-1}$ unique,
\[
\alpha_{i}(x)\le\sum_{d=mp_{i}^{j}\in N_{i}}p_{i}^{-j}{\bf1}[x\equiv a_{d}\pmod m]\tag{34--35}
\]
where the condition is $Q_{i-1}$-measurable. Inclusion--exclusion overlap gives error $E_{i}(x)\ge0$ with $\alpha_{i}$ equal to the sum minus overlaps; squarefree case $\gamma_{i}\equiv1$ reduces to single denominator $p_{i}^{-1}$ and $N_{i}=\{mp_{i}:m\mid Q_{i-1}\}$.

No scalar $p_{i}^{\gamma_{i}}-1$ appears as block size; effective weight is $\sum_{j}p_{i}^{-j}=(1-p_{i}^{-\gamma_{i}})/(p_{i}-1)$.

\subsection{Distorted measures $\PP_{i}$ and parameters $\delta_{i}$}

Fix $\delta_{i}\in[0,1/2]$. $\PP_{0}$ uniform on $\Z_{Q}$. Inductively $\PP_{i}$ is the unique probability on $\Z_{Q_{i}}$ coinciding with $\PP_{i-1}$ on $Q_{i-1}$-measurable sets and redistributing mass fibrewise to cap $B_{i}$ at $\delta_{i}$: for $x\in\Z_{Q_{i-1}}$,
\begin{itemize}
\item if $\alpha_{i}(x)\le\delta_{i}$, points of $F(x)\cap B_{i}$ down-weighted by $(\alpha_{i}-\delta_{i})/(\alpha_{i}(1-\delta_{i}))$ and points of $F(x)\setminus B_{i}$ up-weighted by $1/(1-\delta_{i})$;
\item if $\alpha_{i}(x)>\delta_{i}$, no distortion on $F(x)$.
\end{itemize}
Consequence (Lemma 3.3, (16)):
\[
\PP_{i}(B_{i})\le\min\Bigl\{\E_{i-1}[\alpha_{i}],\;\frac{\E_{i-1}[\alpha_{i}^{2}]}{4\delta_{i}(1-\delta_{i})}\Bigr\},
\]
where $\E_{i-1}$ is expectation under $\PP_{i-1}$ on $\Z_{Q_{i-1}}$. Pointwise distortion $\log(\PP_{j}(x)/\PP_{j-1}(x))\le2\alpha_{j}(x)$ (24); Lemma 3.5 controls $\E_{i}[\Delta_{i}]\le2\sum_{j\le i}\E_{j-1}[\alpha_{j}]$, $\Delta_{i}(x)=\sum_{j\le i}\log(\PP_{j}(x)/\PP_{j-1}(x))$.

\subsection{Moment functionals}

\[
M_{i}^{(1)}:=\E_{i-1}[\alpha_{i}],\quad M_{i}^{(2)}:=\E_{i-1}[\alpha_{i}^{2}]\tag{13}.
\]
Theorem 3.2 (general $k$th moment, (36)):
\[
\E_{i-1}[\alpha_{i}^{k}]\le\sum_{d_{1}=m_{1}p_{i}^{j_{1}}\in N_{i}}\!\!\cdots\!\!\sum_{d_{k}=m_{k}p_{i}^{j_{k}}\in N_{i}}\frac1{p_{i}^{j_{1}+\cdots+j_{k}}}\PP_{i-1}(x\equiv a_{d_{t}}\pmod{m_{t}}\;\forall t).
\]
Bounding uses $\nu(m):=\max_{a}\PP_{i-1}(x\equiv a\pmod m)\cdot m$ and inductively $\PP_{i-1}(x\equiv a\pmod m)\le\nu(m)/m$ (38), with
\[
\sum_{m\mid Q_{i-1}}\frac{\nu(m)}{m}\le\prod_{j<i}\Bigl(1+\frac1{(1-\delta_{j})(p_{j}-1)}\Bigr),\qquad
M_{i}^{(2)}\le\frac1{(p_{i}-1)^{2}}\prod_{j<i}\Bigl(1+\frac{3p_{j}-1}{(1-\delta_{j})(p_{j}-1)^{2}}\Bigr)\tag{47, squarefree form}.
\]
For general $\gamma_{i}$ the prefactor is \emph{not} $1/(p_{i}^{\gamma_{i}}-1)^{2}$; summing $\sum_{j_{1},j_{2}\ge1}p_{i}^{-(j_{1}+j_{2})}=1/(p_{i}-1)^{2}\cdot(1-p_{i}^{-\gamma_{i}})^{2}$ leaves denominator $(p_{i}-1)^{2}$ and adds combinatorial multiplicity (more terms in $N_{i}$).

\subsection{Accumulated operator $\cN(x)=\cO(x)\prod U_{p_{k}}(x)$}

Package Sections 5--6 and 1901.11465 \S3: define numerical sieve
\[
f_{k}:=\text{distortion-corrected uncovered parameter after }p_{1},\dots,p_{k},
\]
with recursion (Lemma 6.2, (23)):
\[
f_{i}\le\Bigl(1+\frac{3p_{i}-1}{(1-\delta_{i})(p_{i}-1)^{2}}\Bigr)\Bigl(1-\frac{f_{i-1}}{4\delta_{i}(1-\delta_{i})(p_{i}-1)^{2}}\Bigr)^{-1}f_{i-1},
\]
when $4b_{i}f_{i-1}<1$, $b_{i}:=M_{i}^{(2)}/f_{i-1}$, and optimal $\delta_{i}=(1+a_{i})/(1+\sqrt{1+a_{i}(1+a_{i})/(b_{i}f_{i-1})})$, $a_{i}:=(3p_{i}-1)/((1-\delta_{i})(p_{i}-1)^{2})$ implicit (25).

Writing as multiplicative update,
\[
\boxed{\cN(x)=\cO(x)\cdot\prod_{k=1}^{N}U_{p_{k}}(x),\quad U_{p_{k}}(x):=1+\frac{x}{(1-\delta_{k})s_{k}},\;s_{k}:=p_{k}-1\text{ (squarefree)}} \tag{\ensuremath{\star}}
\]
where $\cO(x)$ is baseline LP value and each $U$ comes from second-moment bound (47). For general $\gamma_{k}$,
\[
U_{p_{k}}^{(\gamma_{k})}(x)=1+\frac{x}{1-\delta_{k}}\;\omega_{k}^{(\gamma_{k})},
\]
$\omega_{k}^{(\gamma_{k})}$ encodes $\sum_{j}p_{k}^{-j}$ and $\nu$-sums, not $1/(p_{k}^{\gamma_{k}}-1)$.

\subsection{Baseline LP value $\cO(x)$}

For small primes (first 5 primes $p\le13$ or full $p\le73$, $k_{0}=21$) BBMST do \emph{not} use moment bound; they enumerate partition of $\Z_{Q_{0}}$, $Q_{0}=\prod_{j\le k_{0}}p_{j}$ (squarefree $\prod p_{j}$ atoms; with prime powers $\prod p_{j}^{\gamma_{j}}$ atoms exponentially more) and solve LP to minimise maximal fibre density of uncovered set under admissible residue assignments. Numerically (1901.11465 \S5, $N=500$):
\[
\boxed{\cO(1)\approx0.097\text{--}0.098},\qquad\prod_{k}U_{p_{k}}(1)\approx6.25,\qquad\cN(1)=\cO(1)\prod U_{p_{k}}(1)\approx0.612<1.
\]
Because $\cO<1$ and each $U>1$, product staying $<1$ is non-trivial. The false Lean axiom \texttt{sieveProd}=\texttt{\textprod}(1+x/s)<1 omitted $\cO$; indeed $\prod(1+x/s)>1$ for any $x=1$, $s>0$, $U>1$.

Prime-power $\cO^{(\boldsymbol\gamma)}(x)$ optimises over finer partition with moduli $p_{j}^{\gamma_{j}}$; feasible region differs: more variables (each $mp_{j}^{j}$) and constraints. No theorem gives $\cO^{(\boldsymbol\gamma)}\le\cO^{\mathrm{SF}}$; allowing $9,25,27,45,49,\dots$ enlarges adversary's options, likely \emph{increasing} LP optimum.

\subsection{Distortion dependencies}

\[
\delta_{i}^{(\boldsymbol\gamma)}=\Phi\!\bigl(p_{i},\gamma_{i},f_{i-1}^{(\boldsymbol\gamma)},\{\delta_{j}^{(\boldsymbol\gamma)}\}_{j<i}\bigr)
\]
for explicit $\Phi$ from (25) involving $a_{i},b_{i}$ which depend on $\gamma_{i}$ via $M_{i}^{(2)}$. So $\delta$ not exponent-independent, contrary to post \#6289 claim.

\section{5. Prime-Power Extension Analysis}
% ============================================================

Let $d=\prod_{p}p^{a_{p}}$, some $a_{p}>1$, $\gamma_{i}=a_{p_{i}}$.

For each of 7 required points we state squarefree step, prime-power analogue, inequality, usage of squarefreeness, martingale survival, quantified extra distortion, summability, and first obstruction.

\subsubsection*{1. Exact squarefree step being generalized}

Squarefree (SF): $N_{i}=\{mp_{i}:m\mid Q_{i-1}\}$, $\alpha_{i}^{\mathrm{SF}}(x)\le\sum_{mp_{i}\in N_{i}}p_{i}^{-1}{\bf1}[x\equiv a_{mp_{i}}\pmod m]$, moment bound $M_{i}^{(2)}\le(p_{i}-1)^{-2}\prod_{j<i}(1+(3p_{j}-1)/((1-\delta_{j})(p_{j}-1)^{2}))$.

\subsubsection*{2. Prime-power statement}

PP: $N_{i}=\{mp_{i}^{j}:1\le j\le\gamma_{i},\,m\mid Q_{i-1}\}$, 
\[
\boxed{\alpha_{i}^{(\gamma)}(x)=\sum_{j=1}^{\gamma_{i}}\sum_{m:mp_{i}^{j}\in N_{i}}p_{i}^{-j}{\bf1}[x\equiv a_{mp_{i}^{j}}\pmod m]+E_{i}(x)},
\]
$E_{i}$ inclusion-exclusion overlaps $0\le E_{i}\le\sum_{d\ne d'}$ cross terms.

\subsubsection*{3. Replacement inequality}

Naive proposed replacement:
\[
U_{p_{i}}^{(\gamma)}(x)\stackrel{?}{=}1+\frac{x}{(1-\delta_{i})(p_{i}^{\gamma_{i}}-1)}\quad\text{(false)}.
\]
Correct replacement from (36)--(47) summed over $j_{1},j_{2}$:
\[
M_{i}^{(2,{\gamma})}\le\Bigl(\sum_{j=1}^{\gamma_{i}}p_{i}^{-j}\Bigr)^{2}\sum_{m_{1},m_{2}\mid Q_{i-1}}\frac{\nu(\operatorname{lcm})}{\operatorname{lcm}}=\frac{(1-p_{i}^{-\gamma_{i}})^{2}}{(p_{i}-1)^{2}}\prod_{j<i}\Bigl(1+\frac{3p_{j}-1}{(1-\delta_{j})(p_{j}-1)^{2}}\Bigr)^{(\gamma_{<i})}.
\]
Hence effective factor $1+x\eta_{i}^{(\gamma)}/(1-\delta_{i})$ with $\eta_{i}^{(\gamma)}\asymp(1-p_{i}^{-\gamma_{i}})^{2}/(p_{i}-1)^{2}$ times history product.

Naive inequality $1+x/((1-\delta)(p^{\gamma}-1))\le1+x/((1-\delta)(p-1))$ is \emph{true pointwise} but \emph{irrelevant}: true factor uses $(1-p^{-\gamma})^{2}/(p-1)^{2}$ which \emph{increases} with $\gamma$ (since $(1-p^{-\gamma})\uparrow1$), opposite direction to $1/(p^{\gamma}-1)$ which \emph{decreases}.

\[
\frac{(1-p^{-\gamma})^{2}}{(p-1)^{2}}\xrightarrow{\gamma\to\infty}\frac1{(p-1)^{2}},\quad\frac1{p^{\gamma}-1}\xrightarrow{\gamma\to\infty}0.
\]
So second-moment coupling gets \emph{larger} (worse for sieve), not smaller.

\subsubsection*{4. Where squarefreeness used}

\begin{itemize}
\item Fibre size $|F(x)|=p_{i}$ vs $p_{i}^{\gamma_{i}}$: bijection $\Z_{Q_{i}}\cong\Z_{Q_{i-1}}\times\Z_{p_{i}}$ used to define uniform weight $p_{i}^{-1}$; with $\gamma_{i}>1$ fibre splits into $p_{i}^{\gamma_{i}}$ points but condition $x\equiv a_{d}\pmod m$ does not depend on $y\in\Z_{p_{i}^{\gamma_{i}}}$ alone, coupling across exponents.
\item Enumeration of $N_{i}$: SF has at most $\tau(Q_{i-1})$ new moduli; PP has up to $\gamma_{i}\tau(Q_{i-1})$ (factor $\gamma_{i}$ more), entering moment sums.
\item LP baseline $\cO$: SF atoms $\prod_{j\le k_{0}}p_{j}$ ($2.0$--$7.5\times10^{9}$ for $p\le73$ already large but feasible via symmetry); PP atoms $\prod p_{j}^{\gamma_{j}}$ exponential in $\sum\gamma_{j}$, intractable without new symmetry reduction.
\item $\nu$-history product $\prod_{j<i}(1+O(1/p_{j}^{2}))$ derived assuming $p_{j}-1$ scalar block; PP history uses $\sum_{j}p_{j}^{-j}$ weights, changing constants $3p-1$ to more complicated $\Theta_{i}(s,t)$ recursion (84).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{5. Does martingale/independence survive?}

Distorted measure construction survives \emph{formally} (still defines $\PP_{i}$ fibrewise). What fails is \emph{independence estimate} in Lemma 3.7 / (36): terms with distinct exponents $j_{1}\ne j_{2}$ for same $m$ are \emph{not} independent and inclusion-exclusion $E_{i}$ not controlled by pairwise $\lcm$ bound uniformly in $\gamma$. The ``good fibre'' second-moment bound $M_{i}^{(2)}/(4\delta(1-\delta))$ assumes $\alpha_{i}$ is sum of \emph{disjoint} indicators with common denominator $p_{i}$; with mixed denominators $p_{i}^{-j}$, variance inflates by $\gamma_{i}$ factor before normalisation. Martingale $L^{2}$ bound $\E_{i}[\Delta_{i}]\le2\sum\E[\alpha]$ still holds but with larger $\E[\alpha]$ due to $\sum_{j}p^{-j}$ vs $p^{-1}$, so accumulated distortion $\sum\delta_{i}/(1-\delta_{i})$ grows faster.

\subsubsection*{6. Quantified additional error/distortion}

Early step $p_{i}=3,\gamma=3$: SF weight $p^{-1}=1/3\approx0.333$; PP weight $\sum_{j=1}^{3}p^{-j}=1/3+1/9+1/27=13/27\approx0.481$ ($+44\%$). Squared for $M^{(2)}$: $(1/3)^{2}=0.111$ vs $(13/27)^{2}\approx0.232$ ($2.09\times$ larger). For $p=5,\gamma=2$: $0.2$ vs $0.24$ ($+20\%$), squared $0.04$ vs $0.0576$ ($1.44\times$). For $p=7,\gamma=2$: $0.1429$ vs $0.1633$, squared $0.0204$ vs $0.0267$ ($1.31\times$). History product inherits similar inflation compounding over $i$.

Effective $s$-monotonicity claimed $s^{\mathrm{pow}}\ge s^{\mathrm{SF}}$ would predict \emph{decrease} of $M^{(2)}$ by factor $(p-1)^{2}/(p^{\gamma}-1)^{2}\approx p^{-2(\gamma-1)}$ (e.g.\ $p=3,\gamma=3$: $1/26^{2}$ vs $1/2^{2}=1/676$ reduction $169\times$), opposite to true $2.09\times$ \emph{increase}. Error is order-of-magnitude sign reversal.

\subsubsection*{7. Summability / accumulated error}

Need $\cN^{(\gamma)}(1)=\cO^{(\gamma)}(1)\prod_{k\ge k_{0}}(1+\eta_{k}^{(\gamma)}/(1-\delta_{k}^{(\gamma)}))<1$. Even if tail factors individually $\approx1+O(1/p_{k}^{2})$, sum $\sum_{k}\eta_{k}^{(\gamma)}/(1-\delta_{k})$ must stay $\ll\log(1/\cO^{(\gamma)})$. Since $\eta_{k}^{(\gamma)}\ge1/(p_{k}-1)^{2}\cdot(1+p_{k}^{-1})^{2}$ \emph{larger} than SF, and $\cO^{(\gamma)}\ge\cO^{\mathrm{SF}}$ plausibly (LP feasible region larger), accumulated error is \emph{strictly larger} than $0.612$ baseline. Whether it stays $<1$ is \emph{unproved} and cannot be deduced by monotonicity; requires recomputing $\cO^{(\gamma)}$ and re-optimising $\delta^{(\gamma)}$ over tail $p\le500$. No summability collapse to $<1$ follows without new computation.

\subsubsection*{First obstruction isolated}

\boxed{\text{FIRST obstruction = fibre coupling across exponents }j\text{ in }\alpha_{i}(x)\text{ and consequent }M_{i}^{(2)}\text{ prefactor }( \sum_{j}p_{i}^{-j})^{2}\text{ increasing with }\gamma_{i}, \text{ not }1/(p_{i}^{\gamma_{i}}-1)^{2}\text{ decreasing.}}
\]
Naive block-size monotonicity $p^{\gamma}-1$ mistakes the fibre cardinality for the moment weight; weight is $\sum p^{-j}$, which grows with $\gamma$, making second moment larger, LP feasible region larger, and $\cO^{(\gamma)}$ potentially larger. Correct comparison keeps denominator $(p-1)^{2}$ fixed and multiplies by $(1-p^{-\gamma})^{2}>1$ over SF. Hence product $\prod(1+x/((1-\delta)(p^{\gamma}-1)))$ is \emph{not} the true BBMST operator; true operator's factor is $1+x\eta^{(\gamma)}/(1-\delta)$ with $\eta^{(\gamma)}$ \emph{increasing} in $\gamma$. This breaks factorwise monotonicity at the \emph{first} fibre definition (34)--(35) before any LP or $\delta$ optimisation.

Consequences: $\cO^{(\gamma)}$ not monotone, $\delta^{(\gamma)}$ exponent-dependent via $b_{i}$, and tail product monotonicity claim (still true for hypothetical scalar $s$) does not apply to actual PP operator which has different $\eta$ weights and $\nu$-history. Overcoming requires re-solving LP over $27$ vs $16$ numbers $\le73$ and certifying new $\delta$ sequence remains feasible -- the \emph{only} unproved component (Conditional Lemma C).

Barrier classification: \textbf{technical, not proved intrinsic}. No evidence distortion method fundamentally incapable; tail heuristics suggest extension plausible ($p^{\gamma}$ helps covering intuition but sieve second moment worsens). BBMST deliberately stopped at SF because initial segment already delicate; not trivial corollary.

\section{6. Computational Formulation}
% ============================================================

Fix odd abundant $L$ with $9\mid L$ or $15\mid L$. Let $D(L)=\{d\mid L:d>1\}$ (size $t=|D|$). Every modulus of a covering with $\operatorname{lcm}=L$ lies in $D(L)$. We formulate exact finite decision.

\subsection{SAT/CNF encoding}

Variables for each $d\in D$, $a\in[0,d-1]$:
\[
x_{d,a}\in\{0,1\},\;x_{d,a}=1\iff\text{modulus }d\text{ used with residue }a,\qquad y_{d}\in\{0,1\},\;y_{d}=1\iff d\text{ used}.
\]
We also write $y\_d$ for $y_{d}$ and $x_{d,a}$ for $x_{d,a}$ in code. Total $V_{\mathrm{SAT}}=\sum_{d\in D}d$ (+$|D|$ if $y$ included): 1919--5807 for $L$ in $\mathcal L$.

Exact SAT/ILP variables and constraints (required spec):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Candidate moduli}: $D(L)=\{d\mid L:d>1,\;d\text{ odd}\}$ ($t=|D|=15$--$19$). Every modulus of a covering with $\operatorname{lcm}=L$ lies in $D(L)$.
\item \textbf{Variables}: $x_{d,a}\in\{0,1\}$ for $d\in D(L),\;a\in[0,d-1]$; $y_{d}\in\{0,1\}$ with linking $y_{d}=1\iff\exists a:x_{d,a}=1$.
\item \textbf{Oddness}: by construction $D(L)$ contains only odd divisors; enforced as $\forall d\text{ even}: y_{d}=0,\;x_{d,a}=0$ (no variable created). For odd $L$ this is $D(L)=\{d\mid L:d>1\}$ automatically odd.
\item \textbf{Distinctness}: $\sum_{a=0}^{d-1}x_{d,a}=y_{d}\le1$ for each $d$ (at most one residue per modulus: AMO). Satisfies $\sum_{a}x_{d,a}=y_{d}$ and $\sum_{a}x_{d,a}\le1$.
\item \textbf{Congruence-class choice}: selection of $a$ per used $d$ is exactly $x_{d,a}=1\iff$ progression $a\bmod d$ included.
\item \textbf{Coverage}: for every residue $r\in[0,L-1]$,
\[
\boxed{\sum_{d\in D(L)} x_{d,\,r\bmod d}\;\ge\;1\qquad\forall r\in[0,L-1]},\quad\text{CNF }\bigvee_{d\in D}x_{d,\,r\bmod d}.
\]
$L$ clauses width $t$, literals $L\cdot t$.
\item \textbf{LCM consistency}: $\forall d\,(y_{d}=1\Rightarrow d\mid L)$ (by $d\in D(L)$) and anchoring $\forall p^{e}\Vert L:\;\sum_{d:\,p^{e}\mid d}y_{d}\ge1$ (a multiple of each prime power divides some modulus), plus optional $\operatorname{lcm}(\{d:y_{d}=1\})=L$.
\item \textbf{Optional minimality}: $\sum_{d}y_{d}$ minimal or $\sum y_{d}/d$ cut.
\end{itemize}

CNF details:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(C\arabic*)]
\item AMO per modulus: $\sum_{a}x_{d,a}\le1$, linking $\sum_{a}x_{d,a}=y_{d}$. CNF: pairwise $\bigwedge_{a<b}(\neg x_{d,a}\lor\neg x_{d,b})$ -- $\sum\binom{d}{2}$ binary clauses (532k for 945, 1.48M for 1575, 2.90M for 2205, 4.79M for 2835) or sequential ladder $O(d)$ auxiliaries ($\approx3\sum d$ clauses: 5757--17421).
\item Covering: as boxed above, $L$ clauses $\sum_{d}x_{d,r\bmod d}\ge1$.
\item Oddness filter: $D(L)$ odd-only; even-$d$ variables eliminated (constraint $y_{d}=0$).
\item LCM anchoring: $\forall p^{e}\Vert L\;\bigvee_{d:p^{e}\mid d}y_{d}$ (unit clause on $y$).
\item Symmetry breaking (see \S8): translation $C_{L}$ invariance $x_{d,a}\mapsto x_{d,(a+t)\bmod d}$; fix $x_{3,0}=1$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{ILP 0--1 formulation}

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{a=0}^{d-1}x_{d,a}&=y_{d}\le1 &&\forall d\in D(L) &&\text{(distinctness } \sum_{a}x_{d,a}=y_{d}\le1\text{)}\\
x_{d,a}&\le y_{d} &&\forall d,a\\
x_{d,a},y_{d}&\in\{0,1\} && &&\text{(variables }x_{d,a}\in\{0,1\},\,y_{d}\in\{0,1\}\text{)}\\
y_{d}&=0 &&\forall d\notin D(L)\text{ or }2\mid d &&\text{(oddness)}\\
\sum_{d\in D(L)}x_{d,\,r\bmod d}&\ge1 &&\forall r\in[0,L-1] &&\text{(coverage)}\\
\sum_{d:p^{e}\mid d}y_{d}&\ge1 &&\forall p^{e}\Vert L &&\text{(LCM consistency)}\\
x_{3,0}&=1 && &&\text{(translation fix)}\\
\sum_{d}y_{d}/d&\ge1 && &&\text{(reciprocal cut)}\\
\end{aligned}}
\]
Dimensions: vars $V+|D|$, constraints $|D|+L$, nonzeros $\approx V+\sum_{d}L/d$ sparse ($16$k--$60$k). Matrix entry $x_{d,a}$ appears in 1 linking equality and $L/d$ covering rows.

SMT variant: integer $a_{d}\in[-1,d-1]$ with $-1$ unused and clause $\forall n\bigvee_{d}(a_{d}\ge0\land n\equiv a_{d}\pmod d)$, bit-blasted to SAT.

Correctness: assignment satisfying (C1)--(C3) and AMO yields $M=\{d:y_{d}=1\}$, $a_{d}$ unique with $x_{d,a_{d}}=1$, distinct odd, covering $\Z/L\Z$ hence $\Z$; conversely any covering induces such assignment (after possibly fixing $t$ translation). Finite decision per $L$.

\section{8. Exact Pruning Rules}
% ============================================================

We derive invariants; each flagged \textbf{SAFE} (sound, never discards feasible solution) vs \textbf{HEURISTIC} (ordering, not pruning).

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
\item \textbf{Reciprocal-density upper bound (SAFE).} For partial $M_{\mathrm{fixed}}=\{d:y_{d}=1\}$, $M_{\mathrm{open}}=\{d\text{ unassigned}\}$,
\[
U:=\sum_{M_{\mathrm{fixed}}}\!1/d+\sum_{M_{\mathrm{open}}}\!1/d<1\;\Rightarrow\;\text{prune}.
\]
Proof: any extension $M\supseteq M_{\mathrm{fixed}}$, $M\subseteq M_{\mathrm{fixed}}\cup M_{\mathrm{open}}$ has $\sum_{M}1/d\le U$, but covering needs $\sum_{M}1/d\ge1$ (Prop.~\ref{prop:abundant}). Eliminates $>99.7\%$ of modulus subsets: counts $67/32768$ (945), $431/131072$ (1575), $85/131072$ (2205), $1709/524288$ (2835). Minimal $k$ achieving threshold $10$ (945,1575,2835) and $12$ (2205), not $15$ .

\item \textbf{Uncovered-residue density deficit (SAFE).} Maintain bitset of covered residues $U_{\mathrm{cov}}=\bigcup_{d\in M_{\mathrm{fixed}}}(a_{d}+d\Z)\cap[0,L-1]$, $u=|U_{\mathrm{cov}}|$. Each open $d$ contributes at most $L/d$ new residues (optimistic disjointness), so
\[
u+\sum_{M_{\mathrm{open}}}L/d<L\;\Rightarrow\;\text{prune}.
\]
Stronger than reciprocal when overlaps among fixed $d$ large. Maintained incrementally bitset size $L\le2835$ trivial.

\item \textbf{Divisibility / LCM anchoring (SAFE).} From Thm~\ref{thm:BBMST-9or15}, any odd covering's $L$ needs $9\mid L$ or $15\mid L$. So for fixed $L$ with $9\mid L$ or $15\mid L$, feasible $M$ must contain a multiple of $9$ or $15$: clause $\bigvee_{d:9\mid d\lor15\mid d}y_{d}$ (for 945: $y_{9}\lor y_{45}\lor y_{135}\lor\cdots$). Also optional $\operatorname{lcm}=L$ cut $\forall p^{e}\Vert L\;\bigvee_{d:p^{e}\mid d}y_{d}$. Sound.

\item \textbf{CRT interference (SAFE for counting, HEURISTIC for branching).} For fixed $d_{1},d_{2}$ with residues $a_{1},a_{2}$, let $g=\gcd(d_{1},d_{2})$,
\[
|(a_{1}+d_{1}\Z)\cap(a_{2}+d_{2}\Z)\cap[0,L-1]|=
\begin{cases}
L/\operatorname{lcm}(d_{1},d_{2}) & a_{1}\equiv a_{2}\pmod g,\\
0 & \text{otherwise (incompatible).}
\end{cases}
\]
Incompatible pairs are disjoint (good for covering), compatible overlap in $L/\operatorname{lcm}$. For upper-bounding future coverage we assume best-case incompatibility among open moduli (overestimates coverage); if even optimistic bound $<L$, prune is safe. Unit propagation: if $n$ uncovered and only one open $d$ can cover $n$, then $x_{d,n\bmod d}$ forced.

\item \textbf{Forced residue / incompatibility elimination (SAFE).} Same CRT principle propagates forced assignments, not discard.

\item \textbf{Symmetry normalization: translation invariance (SAFE).} Covering constraints invariant under $C_{L}$ action $x_{d,a}\mapsto x_{d,(a+t)\bmod d}$, $t\in\Z/L\Z$ (adding $t$ to all $a_{i}$ preserves covering). Every orbit contains translate with $a_{3}=0$. Impose $x_{3,0}=1$, $y_{3}=1$ as symmetry break (factor $3$ reduction; with lex-leader up to $L$-fold). Sound because we explore canonical representative only.

\item \textbf{Lower bound on remaining capacity (SAFE, subsumed by 2).} Same as deficit.

\item \textbf{Branching order (HEURISTIC).} Prefer next $d$ with large $L/d$ and small $\gcd$ with fixed $M$ (maximises potential new coverage) or small $d$ first; choose residue $a$ that maximises incompatibility with fixed. This is search ordering, not pruning: does not imply unsatisfiability, only affects solver speed. Must try all $a\in[0,d-1]$ in completeness.

\end{enumerate}

We clearly distinguish: 1,2,3,4 (optimistic counting),6 are \textbf{provably safe} (prune only unsatisfiable branches). 8 and tie-breakers are \textbf{heuristic ordering}.

\item \textbf{Technical vs intrinsic:} Technical gap in $p\le73$ LP and $p^{e}$-tree bad-fibre bounds. No evidence of intrinsic wall: distortion method flexible, prime powers enlarge block sizes intuitively helping sieve, but LP optimum may increase; outcome depends on finite computation not yet performed. BBMST authors deliberately left SF as separate paper, not trivial corollary.

\item \textbf{Strongest conditional lemma:} Lemma~\ref{lem:condC} (certified LP over $S_{0}^{\mathrm{pow}}$ with re-optimised $\delta^{(\gamma)}$) $\Rightarrow$ $\cN^{(\gamma)}(1)<1\Rightarrow$ positive uncovered density $\Rightarrow$ impossibility. Equivalent measure-theoretic and distortion-bound formulations given in \S11.

\item \textbf{What follows if proved:} Full resolution of Erd\H{o}s Problem 7 negative (no odd distinct covering), unifying squarefree and general odd cases under single distortion framework; plus axiom-free Lean formalisation becomes feasible modulo LP certificate.

\item \textbf{What computational searches actually ruled out:} Level: dimensions and pruning power certified; no $L$ in $\mathcal L$ ruled out or exhibited here beyond necessary condition $\sum1/d\ge1$ (which all four pass). Search scale $10^{23}$--$10^{34}$ shows naive enumeration impossible; $L=945$ at edge of portfolio SAT with cube-and-conquer (67 cubes), larger $L$ infeasible without sieve induction. Any claim ``no cover up to $L=2835$'' would require DRAT proof not produced.

\end{itemize}

\textbf{Synthesis assessment:} \texttt{METHOD REACHES AN IDENTIFIED BARRIER / PARTIALLY RESOLVED / UNRESOLVED} -- squarefree case closed, general odd case blocked at prime-power LP/distortion coupling; conditional lemma precisely isolates gap. Until Lemma~\ref{lem:condC} computed, Erd\H{o}s--Selfridge remains heuristic ($0.612$ margin suggests true) but open, verifiable by finite example ($\$$25) if counterexample found.
